# PULSE privacy policy and data deletion instructions (draft)

**Draft, not published.** Do not put this at a public address until:

1. every bracket is filled in by the operator;
2. the server it describes runs with `PULSE_RETENTION_DAYS=365`, so that sections 6 and 7 are true (deletion after 12 months and deletion on request were built on 15 Nov 2026; see `pulse-threads-app-review.md`);
3. someone qualified in Indonesian data protection law (UU 27/2022) has read it. This draft was written from what the code does and from Meta's Platform Terms, sections 3.d, 4 and 5 (read 15 Nov 2026). It is not legal advice.

Meta requires the policy at an active, public address that its crawlers can read and that is not blocked by country, and the same address entered in App settings > Basic. It must say what data is processed, how, for what purposes, and how a person can ask for deletion. PULSE may then only process Threads data as the policy describes.

## Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

### 1. Who we are

PULSE is a media monitoring workspace operated by [legal name of the operator], [address] ("we"). It is used by [the communications team of the operator] to follow public conversation about [the topics the team is responsible for].

Contact for anything in this policy: [privacy contact email].

### 2. What this policy covers

It covers two groups of people:

- people whose **public** posts or comments on social platforms and news sites match keywords our team has set up;
- members of our team who sign in to PULSE.

### 3. What we process

**Public posts and comments.** For each post that matches a project's keywords we store its text, its link, the author's public username or channel name, the time it was published, and the public counts the source provides (such as likes, comments, shares and views). For some sources we also store public comments on those posts and, for accounts that posted, the public profile picture and follower count. For accounts on X we also store the location the owner wrote on the public profile and the country or region X names for the account.

**Threads.** We read Threads through Meta's Threads API, with our own Threads account connected. For a matching post we store only its text, its link, the author's username and the time. We do not read or store profile pictures, follower counts, insights or private information from Threads, and we do not publish, reply, repost or send messages through the API.

**YouTube.** PULSE uses YouTube API Services. [Before publishing, add the statements YouTube's developer policies require: a reference to the YouTube Terms of Service and to the Google Privacy Policy. Not checked against YouTube's current text.]

**Team members.** Name, email address, a hash of the password, role, and a log of sign-ins and of changes made in the workspace. Questions typed to the assistant and the conversations kept with it.

We do not collect private messages, contact lists, precise location, or anything that requires signing in to another person's account.

### 4. Why and how we use it

- To show our team what is said publicly about the topics it follows: lists of posts, counts over time, and which sources and accounts speak most.
- To label each post with a sentiment and an emotion, using an AI model, and to write summaries and answers for the team that quote posts and link back to them.
- To produce reports for the team.

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide anything about a person (for example employment, credit, insurance or access to a service). We do not combine Threads data with other data to build a profile of a person.

[Confirm before publishing, and remove this bracket only if it is true:] PULSE is not used for law enforcement or national security purposes.

### 5. Who else handles it

- PULSE runs on servers [at the operator's premises / at provider and country].
- [If a hosted AI service is used:] Post text is sent to [AI service provider] for labelling and summaries, under a written agreement that it processes the text only for us and for no purpose of its own. [If the model runs on our own server, say so instead.]
- Posts from X, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit reach us through [data supplier].
- We disclose data when the law requires it.

### 6. How long we keep it

We keep a stored post, its comments and the labels made from it for 12 months from the day it was published (from the day we collected it when the source gives no date), and then delete them. An account's profile picture, follower count and location are deleted when no post of that account remains. Reports already produced for the team are kept for [period]. Team members' accounts are deleted [period] after they leave the team.

### 7. Your choices

If a post or comment of yours is stored in PULSE, you can ask us to delete or correct it. Write to [privacy contact email] with the link to the post, or with your username and the name of the platform (a comment is found by your username). We delete the stored copies of your posts and comments, the labels and summaries made from them, and your profile picture, follower count and location, within [number] days, and confirm by reply. We also delete on request from the platform.

Deleting or making private a post on the platform does not reach back into copies already stored; ask us as described above.

Team members can ask the workspace administrator to correct or delete their account.

### 8. How we protect it

Only signed-in team members can open PULSE. Access is by role, and sign-ins and changes are logged. [Add what is true for the deployment: encrypted connection, backups, who administers the server.] To report a security problem, write to [security contact email].

### 9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and change the date at the top.

## Data deletion instructions

(For the "User data deletion" field in App settings > Basic. Meta accepts a page with instructions or a callback address; this is the page.)

**How to ask PULSE to delete your data**

1. Write to [privacy contact email] from any address.
2. Give the link to the post, or your username and the platform (for example Threads). Comments are found by username.
3. We delete the stored copy of your posts and comments, the labels and summaries made from them and, where we had them, your profile picture, follower count and location, within [number] days, and reply to confirm.

You do not need a PULSE account to ask. If you had granted the app "Pulse" access to your Threads account, you can remove it at any time in Threads under Settings > Account > Website permissions; tell us and we delete what was collected through that access.
